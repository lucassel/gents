# Uitspraken

What was actually said, and what it means. Nothing else.

There is no "right" column here and no rule underneath. When the speaker says one thing on
Tuesday and another on Thursday, both stay, both dated — the contradiction is data, and it is
usually where the real rule turns out to be hiding. `den deur` and `heel` were both found that
way.

The three files, and they are not the same job:

| file | what it holds |
|---|---|
| `uitspraken.md` | what was said and what it means — the raw material |
| `corrections.md` | the moments a guess was wrong, and what it cost |
| `SKILL.md` | only what is settled enough to act on |

**If you are reading this to derive something: don't.** Take the form that is written here. The
speaker's own instruction, which is why this file exists: *kzout nog nie teveel in regels
proberen duwen* — and *dan vinden we de regel vanzelf wel, moet ie nie zitten raden op basis van
incomplete info van ne gentenaar die zijn eigen ook zit tegen te spreken soms.*

Every line is quoted exactly as it was written, typos and all. Do not tidy them.

## 2026-09-03

| uitspraak | wa et betekent | wa er in zit |
|---|---|---|
| *leest ne keer deze repo pee* | read this repo | imperative **-t**, **ne keer**, address form **pee** |
| *cava?* | how's it going | French *ça va*, written as one word, used as the whole greeting |
| *kwil keer spreken me u dan over 'allez'* | I want to talk to you about *allez* | **me** for *met* — `SKILL.md` has *mee*, and both are now attested from the same speaker |
| *want da is wa ne speciale* | because that one is a bit of a strange one | **wa ne** softener in front of an adjective used as a noun, masculine **-e** |
| *in de heelen file* | in the whole file | **heel** keeps its h; **-en** on the adjective; the article stayed **de** |
| *alsge allez de mcdo is toe zegt isda ook wel klagend* | when you say *allez, McDonald's is shut*, that is complaining | **alsge**, **isda** |
| *allez tis goed voor ene keer* | *allez*, fine, just this once | **ene keer** here, where *ne keer* is the form in the line above — both attested, not resolved |
| *allez?! is idd ongeloof van allez da meende nie?!!* | *allez?!* on its own is disbelief | **idd**; and **van** introducing a quote — *van allez da meende nie* |
| *manee ge zegt ook allez alsge wilt dat gedaan is bvb.* | no — you also say *allez* when you want a thing to be over | **manee** (ma nee, run together), **bvb** |
| *ook als aanmoediging ja van allez tga u lukken* | also as encouragement — *allez, you'll manage* | **tga u lukken**; **van** quoting again, so it is not a one-off |
| *ma allez gast is idd klagend ofzo* | *ma allez gast* is complaining, roughly | **ofzo** — the hedge parked behind a verdict |
| *ge zegt soms ook 'allez en nu ist gedaan e'* | you also say: right, that's enough now | **ist**, and the **e** tag asking for the nod back |
| *ja gast kzout nog nie teveel in regels proberen duwen* | I wouldn't push too much of it into rules | **kzout** = ik zou het, three words in one; **gast** |
| *eerder en soort ledger wel van wa ik zeg en wa dat betekent* | more a ledger of what I say and what it means | **en** for *een*; **van** a third time, here opening the content of the thing |
| *moet ie nie zitten raden* | it shouldn't have to sit there guessing | **ie** for *hij* where `SKILL.md` has *ij*; **zitten + infinitive** as the progressive |
| *die zijn eigen ook zit tegen te spreken soms* | who contradicts himself sometimes too | **zijn eigen** for *zichzelf*, same family as *u eigen uitschijten* |

### Not resolved, and left that way on purpose

Four pairs where the same speaker used both forms. None of these is an error and none of them
needs picking:

- **mee** / **me** — *met*
- **ne keer** / **ene keer** — the second turned up in *voor ene keer*, which may be its own
  fixed phrase rather than a variant
- **ij** / **ie** — *hij*
- **een** / **ne** / **en** — the indefinite article, where *en soort* may also just be a typo

### The one that was nearly missed

**van** as a quotative — *van allez da meende nie*, *van allez tga u lukken*, *van wa ik zeg*.
Three times in one conversation, doing the work English does with *like*. It is in none of the
rules and it never would have been, because nobody thinks to mention it. That is the argument
for this file in one row.
